Dark store shelf planner

Which categories are bought together, and how to lay out a dark store so pickers walk less

In a dark store (an online-only fulfilment store), every extra step a picker takes slows down orders. This project uses 8,902 order lines from 100 customers to find which grocery categories are bought together, then turns that into a store layout you can test and rearrange.

Key findings

  • Pasta and pasta sauce are almost always bought together (r = 0.96).
  • Milk, bread and eggs form a clear breakfast group (r = 0.61 to 0.79).
  • Diapers, wet wipes and chocolates move together (r = 0.58 to 0.78), a parent shopping pattern.
  • Nachos and salsa pair moderately (r = 0.48).
  • The suggested four-aisle layout scores 71 / 100 for togetherness.
